## Hi, I'm Dom 👋

I'm a final-year PhD researcher in Astrophysics at Durham, working on galaxy formation and evolution. 🌌  
Which mostly means working with messy data and trying not to fool myself with it.

### Transition

I'm currently transitioning into data science and quantitative research.  

Turns out the skillset transfers surprisingly well when you swap galaxies for markets — both are noisy, complex, and rarely behave as you'd like.

### What I work on

A lot of my work sits at the intersection of:
- machine learning
- statistical modelling
- building systems to automate research workflows

I’m particularly interested in turning research into something more systematic — less “one good idea”, more repeatable process.

### Current direction

Lately, I've been applying these ideas to financial data — building models and research systems to uncover weak, noisy signals and then stress-testing whether they actually hold up.

(Most don’t. That’s kind of the point.)

### Background

My PhD has involved working with large observational datasets, dealing with uncertainty, and validating results carefully.

A lot of time spent asking: *is this real, or have I just convinced myself it is?*  
Which, as it turns out, is a useful habit to keep.
